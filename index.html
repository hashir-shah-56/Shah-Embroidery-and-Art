OUR JOURNEY & PASSION

16 Years of Turning Threads into Art

Founded by Syeda Tauseefa Abrar, Shah Embroidery & Art is built upon sixteen years of artistic mastery, patience, and passion for handmade textile art.

What started as a personal artistic journey has evolved into a trusted craft atelier, bringing bespoke embroidery, detailed hoop art, and traditional needlework to art lovers around the world.

"To embroider is to weave emotion, memory, and patience into fabric — one stitch at a time."
Syeda Tauseefa Abrar
Owner & Lead Artisan Specialist
